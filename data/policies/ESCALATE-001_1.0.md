# Complaint Escalation Policy

Fictional academic policy — NovaRetail. Version 1.0.

## 9.1 — Urgent escalation
Immediately escalate safety threats to Safety and suspected data exposure to Privacy and Security. Escalation is a review action, not proof of a breach or a promise of compensation.

## 9.2 — Unresolved complaint
Escalate a complaint unresolved after 5 business days to a customer service supervisor. Include the timeline, evidence, attempted actions and unresolved questions.

## 9.3 — Human decision
An authorized employee records the final service decision, rationale and policy version used. AI outputs remain advisory. Never send a customer response or execute a refund solely on an AI recommendation.
