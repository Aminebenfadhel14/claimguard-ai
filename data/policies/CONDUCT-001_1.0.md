# Employee Conduct Policy

Fictional academic policy — NovaRetail. Version 1.0.

## 7.1 — Respectful treatment
Employees must treat customers respectfully and must not insult, threaten or discriminate. Record reported misconduct as an allegation until reviewed with available evidence. Refer alleged misconduct to a supervisor.

## 7.2 — Fair investigation
A supervisor reviews customer statements, employee accounts and lawfully available records. Limit access to the investigation team. Do not issue automatic disciplinary findings from AI output.

## 7.3 — Confidential personnel outcomes
Tell the customer the service resolution and next steps without disclosing confidential personnel sanctions. Personnel decisions remain with authorized human reviewers.
