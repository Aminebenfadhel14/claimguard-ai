# Customer Service Policy

Fictional academic policy — NovaRetail. Version 1.0.

## 1.1 — Acknowledgement
Acknowledge new complaints within 1 business day. Acknowledge the reported experience without presenting unverified allegations as established facts.

## 1.2 — Accessible communication
Offer clear communication in French or English according to customer preference. Explain required evidence in plain language and offer an accessible contact channel when requested.

## 1.3 — Status updates
While an investigation is open, provide a status update at least every 3 business days, including the next step and any missing evidence. Do not invent completion dates.
