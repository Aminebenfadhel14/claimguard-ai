# Billing Policy

Fictional academic policy — NovaRetail. Version 1.0.

## 6.1 — Duplicate charge
For a suspected duplicate charge, Finance compares order ID, payment references, amounts and settlement records. A pending authorization is not necessarily a settled duplicate. Mask payment account details.

## 6.2 — Invoice correction
Correct verified invoice errors through a traceable replacement invoice or credit note. Preserve the original record. Never alter settled transaction history silently.

## 6.3 — Promotional price dispute
Verify the dated promotion, eligible product, channel and stated exclusions before deciding a price dispute. An expired promotion does not automatically apply to a later purchase. Escalate ambiguous advertising to a supervisor.
