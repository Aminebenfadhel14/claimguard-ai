# Delivery Policy

Fictional academic policy — NovaRetail. Version 1.0.

## 5.1 — Missed delivery estimate
A delivery estimate is not proof of delivery. When the estimate is missed, verify the tracking record and open a carrier investigation. Give the customer the next update within 2 business days.

## 5.2 — Lost shipment
A shipment is confirmed lost only after carrier investigation and logistics validation. Offer replacement or refund review after confirmation. A delivered scan disputed by the customer requires investigation, not immediate rejection.

## 5.3 — Transport damage
For suspected transport damage, request photos of the packaging and product and record the delivery date. Keep the claim open while evidence is collected. Do not ask the customer to operate a visibly unsafe device.
