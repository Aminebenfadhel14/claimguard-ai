# Refund Policy

Fictional academic policy — NovaRetail. Version 1.0.

## 3.1 — Refund authorization
Refunds require an approved return, a duplicate payment verified by Finance, or a confirmed lost delivery. An agent proposes the refund; an authorized reviewer approves it. A complaint alone is not approval.

## 3.2 — Refund processing
After approval, Finance initiates the refund to the original payment method within 5 business days. Bank processing may take additional time; do not guarantee a bank settlement date. Send a transaction reference after initiation.

## 3.3 — Partial refund consent
A partial refund or store credit requires documented customer consent and supervisor approval. Never substitute a voucher for an approved monetary refund without consent.
