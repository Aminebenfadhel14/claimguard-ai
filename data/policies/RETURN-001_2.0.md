# Returns and Exchanges

Fictional academic policy — NovaRetail. Version 2.0.

## 4.2 — Defective product exchange
Defective products may be exchanged within 14 calendar days of purchase, subject to proof of purchase and verification of the product condition. Accidental damage and customer misuse are excluded. If eligibility is uncertain, request inspection; do not promise an exchange.

## 4.3 — Change of mind
Unused products in original packaging may be returned within 14 calendar days of purchase with proof of purchase. Opened hygiene accessories and activated digital licences are excluded. This section does not override verified defect claims under section 4.2.

## 4.4 — Exchange stock unavailable
When an eligible defect exchange cannot be completed because the same product is out of stock, offer the customer a refund review under REFUND-001 section 3.1. Do not impose store credit without customer agreement.
