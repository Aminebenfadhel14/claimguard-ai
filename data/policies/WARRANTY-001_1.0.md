# Warranty Policy

Fictional academic policy — NovaRetail. Version 1.0.

## 2.1 — Warranty duration
NovaRetail commercial warranty lasts 12 months from purchase for manufacturing defects. Require proof of purchase and product serial number. This fictional commercial policy makes no statement about statutory consumer rights.

## 2.2 — Warranty exclusions
Commercial warranty excludes accidental impact, liquid damage and unauthorized repairs when they cause the failure. A technician must document the causal finding; an agent must not infer misuse solely from an allegation.

## 2.3 — Repair delay
If an accepted warranty repair exceeds 20 business days after workshop receipt, escalate to the technical supervisor for an alternative resolution review. No automatic refund is implied by this delay.
