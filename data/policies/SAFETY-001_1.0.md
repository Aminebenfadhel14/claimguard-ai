# Security and Safety Policy

Fictional academic policy — NovaRetail. Version 1.0.

## 8.1 — Unsafe battery
For smoke, overheating with swelling, or a suspected battery fire, advise the customer to stop using and charging the device and contact appropriate emergency services if there is immediate danger. Escalate immediately to the safety team. Do not request a powered-on demonstration.

## 8.2 — Recall check
Verify product model and serial number against the current authorized recall register. This document does not list recalled models. Do not declare a model recalled without a register entry.

## 8.3 — Suspected account compromise
For suspected unauthorized account access, route to Security, use the approved identity verification channel and never request passwords or full payment card numbers in complaint text.
