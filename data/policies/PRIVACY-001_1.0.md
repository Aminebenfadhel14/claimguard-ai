# Privacy Policy

Fictional academic policy — NovaRetail. Version 1.0.

## 10.1 — Data minimization
Collect only evidence necessary for the complaint. Mask payment numbers and remove unnecessary personal identifiers from analysis copies. Do not request passwords.

## 10.2 — Access and retention
Restrict complaint records to authorized staff assigned to the case. Retain closed complaint records for 180 days unless a documented legal hold applies, then delete or irreversibly anonymize according to the retention process.

## 10.3 — Privacy requests
Route access or deletion requests to the Privacy team for identity verification and applicable obligation review. Do not promise immediate deletion before checking retention obligations and legal holds.
